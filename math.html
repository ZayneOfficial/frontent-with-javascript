Triangle Congruency Study Guide

Grade 9 Mathematics (CAPS) • ZayneTutor Resources

1. The Axioms of Congruency (≅)

Congruent triangles are identical in both shape and size. All corresponding sides and interior angles are exactly equal. The order of vertices in the congruency statement dictates the matching pairs. If ΔABC ≅ ΔXYZ, then mathematically ∠A = ∠X and AB = XY.

2. The Four Minimum Conditions

Condition Abbreviation Requirements CAPS Reason
Side-Side-Side SSS All three corresponding pairs of sides are equal in length. SSS
Side-Angle-Side SAS Two corresponding sides and the included angle are equal. SAS
Angle-Angle-Side AAS Two corresponding angles and one corresponding side are equal. ∠∠S
Right-Hypotenuse-Side RHS A right angle (90°), the hypotenuse, and one other side are equal. RHS

3. Non-Congruency Traps to Avoid

  • AAA (Angle-Angle-Angle): This proves Similarity (|||), not congruency. The triangles have identical proportions but can be different sizes.
  • SSA (Side-Side-Angle): Having two equal sides and a non-included angle is insufficient to prove congruency unless the angle is exactly 90°.

4. Structuring a Formal CAPS Proof

A valid Grade 9 proof requires declaring the two triangles being analyzed, making three statements of equality with an acceptable geometric reason in brackets, and writing a concluding statement.

Worked Example 1: SAS

Given: In quadrilateral ABCD, diagonal AC bisects ∠A, and side AB = AD.

Prove: ΔABC ≅ ΔADC

Proof:
In ΔABC and ΔADC:
1. AB = AD (Given)
2. ∠A1 = ∠A2 (Given: AC bisects ∠A)
3. AC = AC (Common side)
∴ ΔABC ≅ ΔADC (SAS)

Worked Example 2: RHS

Given: PQ ⊥ QR and TS ⊥ SR. Line PR = TR and QR = SR.

Prove: ΔPQR ≅ ΔTSR

Proof:
In ΔPQR and ΔTSR:
1. ∠Q = ∠S = 90° (Given: PQ ⊥ QR and TS ⊥ SR)
2. PR = TR (Given: Hypotenuses are equal)
3. QR = SR (Given)
∴ ΔPQR ≅ ΔTSR (RHS)
